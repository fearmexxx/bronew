How it works

From a spreadsheet to a private payout in three steps

No CSV templates, no recipient onboarding calls, no public list of who earned what.

Paste the list

One recipient and one amount per line, straight from your sheet. Use .real or .stark names, or plain addresses.

Check every recipient

Each name is resolved and checked before you sign. Bad lines, duplicates and a total above your balance are caught here.

Approve once

Ready recipients are paid privately. Everyone else gets a claim link, funded in the same transaction, that they redeem after a one-time setup.

Privacy, plainly

What stays private and what doesn't

Payments run through Starknet's STRK20 privacy pool. We never see your viewing keys.

Private

  • Who you paid, for direct private transfers
  • How much each person received
  • Your balance and your payout history
  • Who claimed a claim link

Visible on-chain

  • That your wallet used the privacy pool
  • The amount parked behind each claim link, but not who paid it
  • Shielding and unshielding to or from a public address
