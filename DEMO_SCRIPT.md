# Brother ID — three-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:25 — Problem

Wallet addresses are difficult to recognize, while public payment history exposes a user's financial graph. Brother ID connects memorable `.real` names to STRK20 private transfers without taking custody of privacy keys.

## 0:25–0:55 — Product boundary

Show the home page and network control. Explain that `.real` identity registration is currently a Starknet Sepolia beta, while STRK20 shield, transfer, and unshield operations support Mainnet through compatible wallets such as Xverse and Ready.

## 0:55–1:30 — Identity

Search for a name, open the registration modal, and show live contract-derived Sepolia STRK pricing. If connected to Mainnet, demonstrate the explicit switch-to-Sepolia prompt. Do not approve a registration unless you intend to create it.

## 1:30–2:35 — Private STRK

Open Private Wallet, switch to Mainnet, and show that the wallet reports STRK20 support. Demonstrate a small shield, a private transfer to a `.real` recipient, and an unshield. Mention that proof creation occurs inside the wallet and Brother ID never receives the viewing key.

## 2:35–3:00 — Evidence and roadmap

Open the Mainnet transaction in Voyager. Close with the roadmap: launch a narrowly scoped private-payment identity product, measure successful privacy flows and repeat usage, then deploy naming to Mainnet only after security review and validated demand.
